2. CLI Demonstration

Command:

Question:
What precautions are required before entering a robot workcell?

Response:
Before entering a robot workcell, place the robot in a safe state according to the applicable site procedure. Confirm that automatic motion cannot restart unexpectedly. Personnel should use the designated access control and follow lockout/tagout requirements where applicable.

Retrieved source:
industrial_robot_safety.txt


Question:
What should be checked before starting a CNC machine?

Response:
Before starting a CNC machine, check coolant level, lubrication status, workholding, tool condition, enclosure condition, and the work offset/program selected for the job. Confirm that the workpiece is securely clamped.

Retrieved source:
cnc_machine_operation.txt


Question:
What are the symptoms of a PLC communication failure?

Response:
Loss of data exchange, timeout alarms, stale status values, or a device showing an offline state.

plc_maintenance.txt
